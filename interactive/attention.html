The Attention Zoo

Six tabs, two kinds of animation. Five variants of attention are animated on the same decoding loop; the FlashAttention tab is different — it opens up a single attention op and shows tiles of Q, K, and V moving between slow HBM and tiny on-chip SRAM, because Flash changes where the bytes live, not what gets cached. Press Generate and watch tokens appear one by one; every new token writes into the KV cache (the grid), and to be produced at all it must read everything currently cached (the amber sweep) — that read traffic is what decode throughput is made of. Now switch tabs and watch what each design does to the size of the grid. Numbers on the right are real, computed from each mechanism's published configuration.
