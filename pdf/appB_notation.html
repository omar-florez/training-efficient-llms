Notation, conventions, and the running example

The symbols, the constants, and the 7B configuration that every chapter quietly assumes — collected on six pages so no chapter has to restate them

Every chapter of this book leans on a shared set of symbols, a shared bag of constants, and one model configuration that gets rebuilt, sharded, packed, and distilled from Chapter 3 to Chapter 30. Rather than restate them each time, they live here. If you open the book in the middle — which the preface encourages — this appendix is the two-minute orientation that makes any chapter readable.

B.4Conventions

Cross-references "§14.3" point within the book; "Ch. 14" names the chapter. Numeric claims carry footnotes to primary sources; our own measurements are marked as ours and rounded. Every figure is grayscale-plus-navy inline SVG with a numbered caption; code blocks show the lines that matter and point to the companion repository for the rest. Benchmarks are always reported with their harness config per Chapter 9 — a score without its template, shot count, and normalization is treated as a number without units, in this book and in the reader's future reports, which is rather the point of the whole exercise.

Referenced implicitly from every chapter; explicitly from the preface. Corrections: the errata file in the course repository.
