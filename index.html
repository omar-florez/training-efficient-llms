Training Efficient LLMs

A six-month path from engineer to someone who trains large language models — efficiently

Most engineers who want to train language models get stuck in the same place: they can fine-tune a model by following a tutorial, but the space between that and running a real training effort — choosing the data, owning the pipelines, measuring what matters, and not wasting the compute budget — looks like a wall. This program is built to take a working engineer over that wall in six months. This covers: data generation and validation, training and fine-tuning, benchmarking, and model selection.

Words enter, activations climb, the loss fires, gradients descend.

The program

Six months, five phases. Each phase lists what you will do, what you will have built by the end of it, and the materials that support it — book chapters, videos, and references are added here as they are produced.
